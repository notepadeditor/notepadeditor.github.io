Six ways to write

Journal Categories & Starter Prompts

Each category has a purpose. Pick the one that matches what you actually want to capture, then use a starter prompt if you need a nudge.

🙏 Gratitude

What was good today?

Three specific, small things. Not "my family" — the actual moment. Specificity is what makes gratitude entries readable a year later.

Try: "Three small things…"
🎯 Goals

What moved forward?

One thing you actually progressed on, however small. Write down what the next concrete step is while it is still fresh.

Try: "The next step is…"
📚 Learning

What did I learn today?

One insight, stated in your own words. If you cannot explain it in two sentences, you probably have not fully understood it yet.

Try: "In my own words…"
💭 Reflection

What actually happened?

What went the way you expected, and what did not. Reflection entries age well because they record both the facts and your reaction to them.

Try: "I expected X, got Y…"
💡 Ideas

What am I curious about?

Half-formed is fine. Ideas get their value from being recorded early, not from being polished. You can always come back and tag them later.

Try: "What if I…"
🏆 Wins

What went well today?

Not just the big wins. Small moments where you handled something well are exactly what you forget first and benefit most from rereading.

Try: "I handled X well by…"

About privacy

A Journal Nobody Else Can Read

Structured journalling only works if you are honest in what you write, and you are only honest if you trust where it lives. This journal has no backend at all.

Every entry — including its category, rating, favourite flag and tags — is written directly into your browser's local storage. There is no database, no account, no sync server and no analytics reading your text. When you close the tab, your words stay on your machine and nowhere else.

The trade-off is worth stating plainly: because nothing is uploaded, nothing syncs automatically. If you want your journal on a second device, or protection against clearing your browser data, use the Backup .JSON button and move the file yourself.

  • No account, no email, no sign-up
  • Entries never leave your device
  • No ads and no content scanning
  • Works offline once the page has loaded
  • Full export control at all times
